### 4. Technical Quality Standards

1. **Pure Vector-Grade Line Art:** Only pure black pixels (`#000000`) and pure white pixels (`#FFFFFF`). No fuzzy gray anti-aliasing on lines.
2. **Line Hierarchy:** 
   - Heavy outer silhouette lines ($4-6\text{ pt}$) for main landforms, mountain silhouettes, and architectural outlines.
   - Medium contour lines ($2-3\text{ pt}$) for water ripples, tree canopies, and road curves.
   - Fine detail lines ($1-1.5\text{ pt}$) for leaf clusters, wood planks, and brick textures.
3. **Closed Coloring Zones:** All colorable segments must be completely bounded to ensure clean coloring with markers or flood-fill tools without line leaks.
