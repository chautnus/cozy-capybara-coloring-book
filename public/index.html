Amazon KDP & Etsy Production Specs

Mathematical formulas and engineering rules for error-free automated publishing.

Trim Size & Bleed

8.625 × 8.750 in

Trim is 8.5 × 8.5" with 0.125" bleed added to top, bottom, and outside edges (2588 × 2625 px at 300 DPI).

Gutter & Safe Margins

0.500 in Inside Gutter

Keeps illustrations fully visible and flat when opening the book without getting trapped in the spine crease.

Spine Calculation

0.144 in (64 Pages)

Formula: 64 pages × 0.002252" = 0.144". (Under 80 pages: KDP rules dictate spine remains unprinted).

Bleed Shield Architecture

Single-Sided Print

Every odd illustration page is followed by an even dark backing page to prevent alcohol marker bleed-through.
