### 3. Master Checklist of the 30 Non-Repetitive Landmark Scenes

#### ONTARIO (4 Scenes)
1. **Page 01 — Algonquin Provincial Park:** Cedar-strip canoe resting on the quiet lake shore framed by sugar maple canopy *(Sole canoe scene in entire book)*.
2. **Page 02 — Agawa Canyon Scenic Railway:** Vintage passenger train winding along canyon floor beside a sparkling river.
3. **Page 03 — Niagara Falls (Horseshoe Falls):** Grand panorama of the roaring cascade viewed from the historic stone observation promenade.
4. **Page 04 — Rideau Canal & ByWard Market (Ottawa):** Pleasure boat cruising the tranquil canal under red maples with historic stonework.

#### QUEBEC (4 Scenes)
5. **Page 05 — Château Frontenac & Old Quebec:** Majestic castle hotel and cobblestone promenade covered in fallen golden leaves with horse carriage.
6. **Page 06 — Mont-Tremblant Alpine Village:** Colorful pedestrian resort plaza and scenic gondola ascending fiery red mountain slopes.
7. **Page 07 — Montmorency Falls & Suspension Bridge:** 83m waterfall plunge with a dramatic pedestrian suspension bridge crossing over the crest.
8. **Page 08 — Jacques-Cartier National Park:** Deep U-shaped glacial valley with a serpentine river curving through dense maple and birch forests.

#### ALBERTA (4 Scenes)
9. **Page 09 — Moraine Lake & Ten Peaks (Banff):** Monumental snow-capped jagged peaks viewed from the natural rockpile overlooking tranquil turquoise waters *(No canoe)*.
10. **Page 10 — Icefields Parkway Wildlife Vista:** Scenic mountain pass highway with a magnificent bull elk grazing in a golden autumn meadow.
11. **Page 11 — Dinosaur Provincial Park Badlands:** Bizarre mushroom-shaped sandstone Hoodoos and dramatic coulees glowing in autumn light.
12. **Page 12 — Banff Upper Hot Springs:** Steaming open-air mineral pool nestled in the mountain pine forest dusted with first autumn snow.

#### BRITISH COLUMBIA (4 Scenes)
13. **Page 13 — Okanagan Valley Vineyards:** Terraced rows of grapevines with ripening grapes overlooking the sunlit lake and rustic winery estate.
14. **Page 14 — Lynn Canyon Suspension Bridge:** Wooden suspension footbridge swaying high above a rushing river in lush old-growth cedar forest.
15. **Page 15 — Tofino Coastal Rainforest & Pacific Surf:** Wild ocean beach with massive cedar driftwood logs, crashing waves, and misty sea stacks.
16. **Page 16 — Emerald Lake & Natural Bridge (Yoho):** Ancient limestone natural bridge carved by raging turquoise glacier waters.

#### NOVA SCOTIA (2 Scenes)
17. **Page 17 — Cabot Trail & Cape Breton Highlands:** Sweeping highway perched high above the Atlantic Ocean flanked by blazing red maple mountains.
18. **Page 18 — Peggy's Cove Lighthouse:** Red-and-white lighthouse on smooth glacial granite boulders with rolling ocean breakers.

#### NEW BRUNSWICK (2 Scenes)
19. **Page 19 — Hopewell Rocks (Bay of Fundy):** Massive flowerpot sandstone sea stacks standing on the exposed seabed at extreme low tide.
20. **Page 20 — Dickson Falls (Fundy National Park):** Stepped cascade rushing down mossy boulders with an intricate wooden viewing footbridge.

#### PRINCE EDWARD ISLAND (2 Scenes)
21. **Page 21 — Cavendish Beach & Red Sandstone Cliffs:** Striking red ocean cliffs contrasting with golden marram grass dunes and ocean waves.
22. **Page 22 — Point Prim Lighthouse:** Prince Edward Island's oldest circular brick lighthouse (1845) on a breezy coastal headland.

#### NEWFOUNDLAND & LABRADOR (3 Scenes)
23. **Page 23 — Western Brook Pond Fjord (Gros Morne):** Monumental inland freshwater fjord framed by 2,000-foot vertical granite cliffs.
24. **Page 24 — Quidi Vidi Fishing Village (St. John's):** Picturesque wooden fishing stages on stilts and small boats in a sheltered rocky cove.
25. **Page 25 — Cape Spear Lighthouse:** The easternmost point of continental North America on windswept oceanic bluffs.

#### THE PRAIRIES (Manitoba & Saskatchewan - 3 Scenes)
26. **Page 26 — Riding Mountain Ominik Marsh (Manitoba):** S-curved floating boardwalk trail navigating through cattails, lily pads, and golden birch groves.
27. **Page 27 — Churchill Tundra & Autumn Aurora (Manitoba):** Polar bear mother and cub traversing the fiery red autumn tundra under dancing northern lights.
28. **Page 28 — Prairie Grain Elevators (Saskatchewan):** Iconic vintage wooden grain elevators rising above vast golden wheat fields and a classic tractor.

#### THE NORTHERN TERRITORIES (Yukon & NWT - 2 Scenes)
29. **Page 29 — Tombstone Mountain & Arctic Tundra (Yukon):** Crimson-carpeted tundra plain leading to the razor-sharp black granite spire of Tombstone Peak.
30. **Page 30 — Alexandra Falls (Twin Falls Gorge, NWT):** Roaring 32-meter limestone waterfall curtain framed by amber northern birches and pines.
